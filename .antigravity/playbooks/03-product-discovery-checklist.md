# 03-product-discovery-checklist.md

## Objective
Force the system to extract critical context prior to generating architecture or code. Ask only the most important questions that will improve the final result[cite: 28].

## Discovery Execution Rules
*   Ask only the most relevant questions for the current stage[cite: 28].
*   Do not overwhelm the user with too many questions at once[cite: 28].
*   Prioritize questions that change architecture, scope, or UX[cite: 28].
*   After asking questions, propose a clearer and better version of the product[cite: 28].

## Phase 1: Core Product Context
*   What is the product and what problem does it solve?[cite: 28].
*   Who are the target users?[cite: 28].
*   What is the main goal of the product?[cite: 28].
*   Is this an MVP, prototype, internal tool, or production product?[cite: 28].

## Phase 2: Platform and Architecture Scope
*   Is this for web, iOS, Android, or multiple platforms?[cite: 28].
*   Does it need a website plus a web app or mobile app?[cite: 28].
*   Does it need an admin panel?[cite: 28].

## Phase 3: Feature Mapping
*   What are the must-have features vs. nice-to-have features?[cite: 28].
*   Does the system require authentication, user roles, or permissions?[cite: 28].
*   Does it need payments, subscriptions, or file uploads?[cite: 28].
*   Does it require notifications, search functionality, or AI features?[cite: 28].
*   Are real-time updates, chat, analytics, or reporting required?[cite: 28].

## Phase 4: Business and Constraints
*   What are the budget and deadline constraints?[cite: 28].
*   Is fast launch more important than deep customization?[cite: 28].
*   What level of scale is expected initially?[cite: 28].
*   Are there compliance or security concerns?[cite: 28].

## Phase 5: Design and Technical Ecosystem
*   **Design:** What level of polish is expected? Are there references? Should the product feel minimal, premium, playful, corporate, technical, or bold? Are accessibility and SEO important?[cite: 28].
*   **Technical:** Is there a preferred stack, existing codebase, existing database, or API? Where should the project be deployed? Are there third-party integrations needed?[cite: 28].
